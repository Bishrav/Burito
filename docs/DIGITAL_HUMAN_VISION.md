# Digital human vision

Burito is a local-first digital companion for the owner. It should communicate
naturally, reason through unfamiliar problems, teach, research, assist with
software development, manage knowledge, and perform authorized computer work.
“Digital human” describes the desired range of interaction and utility. It
does not imply biological consciousness, emotions, or artificial general
intelligence.

The Ryoku CachyOS desktop is the inference and execution host. It uses
Hyprland on Wayland, an i9-14900K, an RTX 5070 with 12 GB VRAM, 32 GB nominal
RAM, and internal NVMe. The intended portable brain is the existing 4 TB
Transcend HDD. Preserve Windows, boot configuration, and Ryoku storage and
customization. The Apple Silicon MacBook and iPhone are planned companions.

## How the system earns autonomy

Observe relevant data, infer the objective, clarify important gaps, retrieve
evidence, compare plans, and identify prerequisites and risks. Obtain authorization,
execute with scoped tools, observe the result, and verify it against the objective.
Revise within explicit time, retry and resource budgets, or report a blocked
state. Save reusable learning only within approved scope. Report plans, attempts
and verified completion distinctly.

| Level | Permitted operating mode |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | Conversation |
| 1 | Authorized read-only observation |
| 2 | Recommendations and plans |
| 3 | Approval-controlled execution |
| 4 | Preapproved, tested, narrowly scoped skills |
| 5 | Bounded delegated multi-step workflows |
| 6 | Opt-in proactive preparation and assistance |

The executor owns permissions outside the model. Scopes must support expiry,
revocation, cancellation, audit, emergency stop and resource limits. No specialist
can expand its own authority. Prefer structured APIs, accessibility and typed
tools over visual coordinates. Keep approval for consequential commands, account
changes, purchases, submissions and outbound messages. Never collect credentials,
bypass authentication, run unrestricted root actions, silently export private
data, monitor covertly, or deploy uncontrolled self-modification.

## Long-term capabilities

Maintain continuity through task checkpoints, across sessions and paired devices.
Explain the sources and uncertainty of memories. Build an authorized workspace
model for project context and opt-in preparation. Compare alternatives and failure
scenarios before action. Diagnose runtime problems and use measured failures to
improve skills. Learn from owner-approved demonstrations with safe validation,
versioning and rollback. Route models by privacy, complexity, latency, cost and
resources, with local inference the default. Keep portable knowledge and skills
independent of one model or OS. Propose research gaps, test competing explanations,
and challenge assumptions respectfully. Evaluate self-improvement in isolation
before any approved deployment.

Voice and vision start with push-to-talk and visibly authorized screen access.
Pause, mute, exclusion and emergency controls come before always-available
features. Mobile pairing, encrypted transfers, locked-PC non-GUI tasks, device
revocation and Wake-on-LAN need separate verification. Keep model and unrestricted
control interfaces off the public internet. Durable automation needs checkpoints,
retry limits, cancellation and completion evidence before delegation to any
specialist agents.
